# Inventory 2-b-2 asset audit

Status: deprecated historical comparison; active 2-b is `../2-b/`.

## Asset decision

2-b-2 changes only the placement and sizing of existing UI areas. It forks the
accepted 2-b asset set without adding, replacing, recoloring, or regenerating
any image asset.

All provenance, production cautions, silhouette restrictions, and corrected
M16/M249/P1911 socket semantics remain authoritative in
`../2-b [Deprecated]/ASSET-AUDIT.md`.

## Unreal handoff

The reusable widget files and their bindings are unchanged from 2-b. UMG layout
deltas are the CanvasPanel placement of the tooltip, Vicinity, capacity bar,
and weapon rack plus the Gear/Outfit and character-preview sizing recorded in
`IMPLEMENTATION-SPEC.md`.
